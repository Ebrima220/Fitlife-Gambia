About fitlife Gambia

Our story,mission and team behind your fitness journey.

Our Story

fitlife Gambia was found in 2019 by fitness enthusiasts who saw a need for modern, inclusive gym in the Kololi area. What started as a small studio with basic equipment has grown into one of the most popular fitness center in the region

We beiliev that witness should be accessible to everyone, regarding of their experiance level. whether you are taking your first steps into fitness or you you are a seassoned athlet, Fitlife has somthing for you

Our facility is located on the Senegambia Highway, easily accessible to both locals and visitors staying in the tourism area. we've become a community hub where people come not just to workout, but to connect, motivate, each other, and grow together

Our Mission

To empower every indevidual in The Gambia to live a healthier and stronger and more confident life through fitness education, quality facilities and supportive community
